The Geography of Waiting

In 2025, Philadelphia residents submitted 27,987 reports of abandoned vehicles. What happened after they clicked “submit” varied across the city.

Does where someone lives shape how long a 311 case remains unresolved?

05

What this map can—and cannot—tell us

Philadelphia’s 311 records reveal an uneven geography of administrative waiting. They do not, by themselves, explain why those differences exist.

  • The data include reported problems, not every abandoned vehicle.
  • Residents may differ in how often they use 311.
  • A “closed” case does not necessarily mean the vehicle was removed.
  • The data snapshot was last updated September 12, 2026.

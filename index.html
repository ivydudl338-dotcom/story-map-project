Philadelphia 311 · Case file 2025

The Geography
of Waiting

A resident reports one abandoned vehicle. Across Philadelphia, that action was repeated 27,987 times in 2025.

When thousands of individual reports enter the same system, where does waiting accumulate?

06 / TESTING AN EXPLANATION

Do more reports automatically mean worse outcomes?

Not on their own. ZIP Codes with more requests did not consistently have lower on-time completion rates.

Each circle is a ZIP Code. Highlighted: 19128 and 19131.

This is a simple association, not a causal test. Case complexity, staffing, towing capacity, repeat reports, and other factors are not measured here.

08 / WHAT THE RECORD LEAVES BEHIND

A shared deadline.
Uneven outcomes.

Philadelphia gave residents a common service expectation, but the likelihood of meeting it varied across the city.

The geography of waiting is not only a map of delay. It is a map of where a city's own expectations became harder to reach.
Methods and limitations
  • The records describe reported problems, not every abandoned vehicle.
  • Multiple requests may refer to the same vehicle.
  • “Closed” does not necessarily mean that a vehicle was removed.
  • “Open” does not necessarily mean that a vehicle remained at the location.
  • The data cannot establish why ZIP Code outcomes differ.
  • The dataset snapshot was last updated September 12, 2026.
